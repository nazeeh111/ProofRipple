First public research prototype: finite implication consequence discovery,
seed-dependent proof witnesses, a trained CPU usefulness ensemble, five ranking
policies, frozen evaluation with negative findings, independent pinned Lean
checks and empty-axiom audits, CLI/Python integration, and PROOFRIPPLE LAB.

The demo improves bounded downstream success from 1/4 to 4/4. The full benchmark
does not show learned superiority over its graph heuristic. The scope excludes
arbitrary Lean/Mathlib imports, global novelty and new mathematical discoveries.

# Changelog

## 0.3.1 · 2026-10-29

Reject malformed imported presentation data before changing the active run.
Covers ranking metadata, witness paths, solved flags, set/proof trees, templates
and audit containers, with regression checks for all three recorded domains.

Include the frozen evidence, proof sources and linked reproduction drivers in
the wheel. Serve compressed audits and read-only sources, provide browsable
evidence directories, and restore the existing Scope anchor. CI now checks
installed documentation/evidence completeness and the import contract.

Requires setuptools 77.0.3+ to match the SPDX license-expression metadata.

The eight frozen scientific modules, v0.3 study/model/data, original failed
controls and separate repaired-control evidence remain byte-identical.

## 0.3.0 · 2026-10-29

Adds pinned Mathlib set algebra with multi-premise lattice proofs, strict typed
imports, a compact dark LAB, actual proof trees and evidence disclosures.

Expands to 24 held-out contexts and 254 goals with nine fixed budget profiles,
ten policies, portfolio-conditioned learned ranking and stronger dynamic
heuristics. The primary learned-minus-greedy result is −0.64 percentage points
(95% paired context interval −1.55 to +0.20): no clear learned advantage.
All 1,997 expected declaration audits pass. A separately documented
post-study preprocessing repair lets ordinary automation prove 254/254 goals;
original failed controls and frozen ranking outcomes remain unchanged.

Includes frozen sources/data, independent artifact and proof replay, targeted
Mathlib setup, reproducible CI and fresh interface media. Earlier domains and
release evidence remain preserved.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-26

Adds actual Lean list-capacity proofs, a registered declaration importer, typed
AST inputs, concrete theorem exports, explicit domain axiom policies, and a
strong independent automation control. Extends ranking with typed symbolic
embeddings and frozen proof-reuse features, with a structure-only ablation.

Adds common list-identity normalization and independent rule/implementation
review. A leaking preliminary corpus experiment was rejected and rebuilt; its
numbers are not release results. The compact natural example shows 0/2→1/2
bounded search success with one graph-selected consequence. Strong Lean
automation solves the same showcase without discovered lemmas. Sparse corpus
results and ranking limitations are reported in full.

The LAB adds domain/policy controls, editable lemma budgets and imported theorem
audits. The original propositional domain keeps its strict axiom-free policy.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

First public research prototype: finite implication consequence discovery,
seed-dependent proof witnesses, a trained CPU usefulness ensemble, five ranking
policies, frozen evaluation with negative findings, independent pinned Lean
checks and empty-axiom audits, CLI/Python integration, and PROOFRIPPLE LAB.

The demo improves bounded downstream success from 1/4 to 4/4. The full benchmark
does not show learned superiority over its graph heuristic. The scope excludes
arbitrary Lean/Mathlib imports, global novelty and new mathematical discoveries.
